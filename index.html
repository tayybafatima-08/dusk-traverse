Sol 4, NASA data

Built on a laser map of the whole planet.

NASA's MOLA elevation gives the slope of 259,200 cells across Mars. Our path finder uses those slopes to choose the safest route.

Base maps: NASA Mars Trek. Site hazard ratings are our own estimates.

Test every traverse before a single wheel turns.
